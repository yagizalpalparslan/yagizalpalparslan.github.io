About Me

I’m a gameplay developer focused on building interactive 3D gameplay systems with Unity and C#. I work on player controls, game mechanics, object interactions, UI logic, and gameplay features that make projects feel responsive and playable.

Alongside Unity, I also have experience creating Unreal Engine projects using Blueprints, where I develop prototype mechanics, interaction systems, and level-based gameplay logic.

I’m more focused on the technical side of game development than game design. I enjoy turning gameplay ideas into working systems, solving technical problems, writing clean and functional code, and improving how the game feels through iteration.

I develop gameplay systems for Unity 3D projects, including player movement, camera behavior, object interactions, scoring systems, UI logic, and core game mechanics. I also create gameplay prototypes in Unreal Engine using Blueprints.

My focus is on coding, implementation, and making gameplay systems work smoothly inside the engine.

3D Final Project - Garden and Dog Modeling

View details and images

https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/2rijHfMTy1Y 3D Final Project - Garden and Dog Modeling

For this project, I created a 3D model of my dog and our garden in Blender. My goal was to design a personal environment that could be used both in my academic work and potentially in one of my own future game projects.

I used the real garden and my dog as references, while also adding my own creative interpretation to the scene. I focused on capturing the overall structure of the environment and the recognizable characteristics of my dog in a stylized way.

Through this project, I gained experience in environment modeling, organic modeling, scene composition, material creation, and lighting. It also helped me practice transforming a real-life location into a game-ready 3D environment.

3D Midterm Project - Cars-Inspired Hotel and Room Design

For this project, I modeled a hotel inspired by the world of Cars, along with the interior of one of its rooms.

While designing the scene, I aimed to preserve the visual identity and atmosphere of the movie while adding my own interpretation to the environment. I used pre-made models of Lightning McQueen and Sally and integrated them into the scene to support the composition and overall theme.

This project allowed me to improve my skills in architectural modeling, interior design, scene composition, lighting, and the integration of pre-made assets into an original environment.

Limi’s Grimes - Narrative Design Project

Limi’s Grimes is a narrative-driven horror game concept set in rural America in 1977. The story follows a group of young characters exploring an abandoned uranium mine and later shifts to a CIA agent investigating their disappearance.

The project features branching choices, hidden narrative variables, multiple endings, environmental storytelling, character development, gameplay integration, and detailed scene scripting.

My main responsibilities included editing the prologue and main storyline, improving narrative consistency, and developing the characters and their personalities.
